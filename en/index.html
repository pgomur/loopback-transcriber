LoopbackTranscriber

Windows desktop application · Open source (MIT)

Record, transcribe and summarize your meetings without anything leaving your computer.

It records the audio of one specific application (Teams, Zoom, Meet in the browser…) together with your microphone. It then transcribes it with Whisper, tells what you say apart from what the others say, and, when you ask for it, writes a summary with a language model. While you record, it can also show the meeting translated in a floating window.

Getting started How it works inside GitHub
  • System 64-bit Windows 11 10.0.22000 or later
  • Acceleration Vulkan GPU Without one, CPU (slower)
  • Transcription Whisper large-v3-turbo ~624 MB model
  • Summary and translation Qwen3-4B ~2.5 GB model
  • Internet Only to download models Everything else is local
  • Built with WPF · .NET 10 MVVM pattern
  • Languages ES EN PT DE RU Interface, meetings (or automatic detection) and translation

How to use it

Optional: open live translation to follow the meeting translated while you record.

  1. Pick the application

    Teams is built in. Zoom, the browser or others are added in Applications.

    See the guide

  2. Record

    Press Start recording (or use the tray icon). The application, the default audio output and your microphone are recorded at the same time.

    See the guide

  3. Get the transcript

    When you press Stop recording, Whisper transcribes it and, if your microphone was recorded, separates Participants from You. It is saved in the history.

    See the guide

  4. Summarize

    In the AI summary tab, press Summarize: title, key points, goals and next steps.

    See the guide

Features

Each feature links to its user guide and its technical explanation.

Per-application recording

Only the audio of the chosen program, plus your microphone on a separate track. If that track turns out silent, it uses the default audio output.

Guide · Technical

Any application

Teams is built in. Add Zoom, Discord or the browser (for Meet) from the processes that are playing audio.

Guide · Technical

Transcription with Whisper

Whisper large-v3-turbo, on a Vulkan GPU when there is one. Fixed language or automatic detection. Filters hallucinations, repetitions and echo.

Guide · Technical

Who is speaking

Your microphone is transcribed separately and aligned with the meeting. Each paragraph says Participants or You, with your name in front if you set one.

Guide · Technical

History and search

Search the title, application, date, summary and full text, ignoring case and accents. Filter by date and duration, and transcribe again with the current settings.

Guide · Technical

AI summary

Qwen3-4B writes a title, purpose, key points, topics, goals and next steps, in the application language.

Guide · Technical

Live translation

Floating window with what the participants say and its translation into Spanish, English, Portuguese, German or Russian.

Guide · Technical

Nothing is lost

If something goes wrong (an error, an exit, a shutdown or a crash), the audio is kept as an unprocessed recording so it can be retried.

Guide · Technical

Privacy and data

What uses the internet and where your files end up.

Everything stays on your computer

Audio, transcripts and summaries never leave it. The application only goes online to download the two models, from a fixed Hugging Face revision and checking their SHA256. The installer may also download Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable if it is missing.

Only record with the consent of the people taking part.

Models and integrity

Where everything is stored

In %LocalAppData%\LoopbackTranscriber: settings, models, transcripts, unprocessed recordings and log. The models and transcripts folders can be changed in Settings.

Each recording is a folder with the text in Markdown (.md) and the audio (.wav), plus, when there are any, your microphone (_mic.wav) and the summary (.summary.json).
